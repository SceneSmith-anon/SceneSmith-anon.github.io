SceneSmith:
Agentic Generation of Simulation-Ready Indoor Scenes

Anonymous Authors
arXiv Video Code Scene Dataset

Fully automated text-to-scene generation. This entire community center was generated by SceneSmith without any human intervention, from a single 151-word text prompt. Beyond explicitly specified elements, SceneSmith places additional objects from inferred contextual information, such as ping pong paddles and balls placed near a ping pong table. Objects are generated on-demand, are fully separable (non-composite), and include estimated physical properties, enabling direct interaction within a simulation. The resulting scenes are immediately usable in arbitrary physics simulators (robots added for demonstration).

Scene Construction

“A dining room with a long table set for 12 people…”

“A bookstore with…”

“A cafe with a bar counter, small round tables, and a chalkboard menu…”

“A robotics lab with workbenches, robotic arms, and a 3D printer…”

“A yoga studio with yoga mats, a wall mirror, and meditation cushions…”

SceneSmith constructs scenes through five successive stages, each implemented as an interaction among three VLM agents: a designer that proposes object placements, a critic that evaluates realism and coherence, and an orchestrator that mediates between the two. The stages are (1) Layout, generating the architectural layout; (2) Furniture, placing furniture objects; (3) Wall-mounted objects, adding items such as shelves, mirrors, and art; (4) Ceiling-mounted objects, adding items such as lights and fans; (5) Manipulands, populating surfaces with small manipulable objects.
